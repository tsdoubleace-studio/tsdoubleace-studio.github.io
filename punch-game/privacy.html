LaughLoop Punch Game – Privacy Policy

Developer: TsDoubleAce, in partnership with LaughLoop Studio · Effective date: 14 November 2026

LaughLoop Punch Game ("the game") is a cartoon game for children and families starring the LaughLoop Studio characters, published on Google Play by TsDoubleAce ("we"). We care about children's privacy. This policy explains what happens to information when you play.

The short version

  • We do not ask for, collect, store or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no analytics.
  • The free version shows one small banner ad from Google AdMob, set up for children: only family-friendly (G-rated), non-personalised ads, and the game does not use the advertising ID.
  • The one-time "Full Game" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, stars, stickers, gloves, settings and language) and any funny faces you draw in the game are saved only on your device. It is never sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

The game does not use the camera, the microphone, your location, your contacts or your photos.

Ads (free version)

The free version shows a banner ad from Google AdMob at the top of the menu screens and the bonus games. It is never shown during normal levels. We tell Google that the game is directed to children (child-directed treatment and "under the age of consent"), and we limit ads to the G content rating. This means Google shows only non-personalised ads, does not build an advertising profile, and does not use the device's advertising ID (the game removes that permission).

To show and count ads and to prevent fraud, Google may process limited technical information such as the device's IP address, the device type and operating system, and whether an ad was shown or tapped. Google processes this under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information in ads. We never receive personal information from Google about you or your child.

Buying the Full Game removes all ads.

Purchases

The game offers one optional in-app purchase, "Full Game", which removes the ads and opens every level and all bonus games. Purchases are made through Google Play and are protected by a grown-ups check inside the game. Payment details are handled only by Google; we do not see or store them. The game asks Google Play whether the purchase was made so it can unlock it on your devices.

Links outside the game

A "LaughLoop on YouTube" button opens our YouTube channel in the YouTube app or a browser. It is protected by a grown-ups check. Once you leave the game, YouTube's own terms and privacy policy apply.

Permissions

  • Vibration – a short buzz when you punch (can be turned off in Settings).
  • Internet and network state – only to load the banner ad and to check the Google Play purchase.

Children's privacy

The game is designed for children and complies with the Google Play Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country). If you believe information about a child has been shared with us, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
